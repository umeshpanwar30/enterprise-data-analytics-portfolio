# Corporate Finance Cash-Flow & Margin Optimization Hub 📊💼

An enterprise financial intelligence framework that tracks multi-month cash flows, monitors operating expenditures, and measures actual net profit margins against corporate budget targets using advanced DAX modeling.

---

## 🛠️ Architectural Blueprint
- **Relational Data Warehouse (SQL):** Managed transactional cash ledgers (`finance_cash_ledger`) and operating targets (`monthly_operating_budgets`) inside PostgreSQL.
- **Advanced DAX & Margin Engineering:** Engineered semantic measures using DAX to isolate incoming gross revenue from operating expenses, marketing loads, and taxation outflows, computing real-time Net Profit (£13.98K) and Net Margin percentages (46.29%).
- **Presentation Matrix (Power BI):** Designed target variance speedometer gauges and chronological year-month bar charts to evaluate monthly actual performance margins against executive corporate plan goals.

---

## 📁 Repository Structure
- `schema.sql`: Contains transactional ledger tables, constraints, and 2026 Q1 financial simulation data.
- `Corporate Finance Cash-Flow & Margin Optimization Hub.pbix`: Executive financial modeling and Power BI dashboard file.
